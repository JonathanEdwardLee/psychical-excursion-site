# Exercise-pause policy

The audiobook is a spoken book, not a timed meditation product.

## Three pause classes

| Class | Cue in session scripts | Spoken? | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary punctuation | none | punctuation already in prose | commas, periods, paragraph breaks |
| Short / reflective | `<!-- cue:short-pause -->` or `<!-- cue:reflective-pause -->` | no | after a heading, punch line, or a question the listener can notice |
| Optional practice silence | `<!-- cue:optional-practice-pause -->` | no | after a line that already asks the listener to remain still or begin sleep |

Cues are HTML comments. They are stripped from spoken word counts. They must never be read aloud.

## What not to do

- Do not insert long timed silences that stall a normal commute listen.
- Do not add narrator lines such as “take a deep breath with me” unless the Publication Master already says that.
- Do not change canonical chapter prose to solve pacing.

## If a practice needs more time

Prefer, in order:

1. A brief natural pause in the main chapter track.
2. The listener pausing the player. If a later production pass needs an explicit reminder, speak **Pause the audio here if you want more time** as a credits-or-pilot overlay — not as a rewrite of the book.
3. An optional companion practice track recorded later from the same session script, not a second manuscript.

Relax the body, Intention, and sleep-onset watches stay in the chapter that already contains them.
