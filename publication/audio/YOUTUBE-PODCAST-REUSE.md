# YouTube and podcast reuse

Record the chapter tracks as reusable core narration.

## Same files

Chapter session scripts are the audiobook performance. Later video or podcast episodes should layer extra audio **around** those files, not rerecord the investigation.

## Add later, do not bake in

- Episode intro/outro (“this is a reading from Psychical Excursion, chapter ten”)
- Link spoken as psychicalexcursion.com plus the chapter’s public path, in the **wrapper**, not in the audiobook master
- Artwork, captions, sky-clock stills, or a static title card
- Platform subscribe reminders

## Do not bake into canonical audiobook audio

- “Like and subscribe”
- Retailer names
- Limited-time prices
- A second rewritten lecture version of the chapter

Credits tracks (`PEX-AUDIO-00` / `PEX-AUDIO-99`) may be omitted or replaced on YouTube/podcast if those platforms need a shorter bumper. Chapter files stay intact.
