# Narration guide

Same Publication Master as print/ebook. Do not maintain a second rewritten novel.

## Voice

Curious. Conversational. Mildly amused when the manuscript is amused. Precise around evidence. Calm in exercises.

Not a mystical announcer. Not a meditation-app whisper. Not paranormal certainty.

Preserve Jonathan's humor and short punch lines. Do not add audiobook filler or “as you heard earlier.”

## Protected lines — read exactly

**My body sleeps. I remain aware. I recognize the transition and calmly enter.**

**I stay with the experience and explore before I explain.**

**Next time I am dreaming, I remember that I am dreaming.**

## Do not read aloud

- YAML front matter in chapter files
- Inline `[n]` citation markers (leave them in the master; skip them while speaking)
- `## References` lists, DOIs, PMIDs, URLs
- This audio folder
- Production notes below

A single standard line may be read once in front or back matter:

**Full source notes appear in the print and ebook editions.**

## Production cues (not in book prose)

Keep cues in generated session scripts under `audio/session-scripts/`, not in `chapters/`. See `PAUSE-POLICY.md`. Regenerator: `npm run generate:session-scripts`.

| Cue | Use |
| --- | --- |
| short pause | After a punch line or between experiment steps |
| long pause | Optional exercise silence; do not invent a timed meditation unless the chapter already asks the listener to wait |
| chapter start/end | Title, then a breath |
| do not read references | End the chapter at Intention |

No TTS vendor or cloned voice is specified in this pass.

## Exercises

Read exercise instructions as the book writes them. Do not convert them into generic guided-meditation scripts. Optional silence after “remain still” or “notice” is allowed; duration is the narrator's judgment, not a product requirement.
