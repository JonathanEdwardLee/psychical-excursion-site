Sleep and safety.

<!-- cue:short-pause -->

Sleep comes first.

Do not treat sleep loss, forced sleep paralysis, or stacked nightly interruption as commitment. Wake-back-to-bed is optional. Vibrations are not required. Sleep paralysis is not required.

If a practice leaves you exhausted, frightened, or unable to sleep, simplify it or stop.

This book is not medical advice. It does not diagnose sleep disorders or treat them.
