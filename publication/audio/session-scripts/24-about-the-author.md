About the author

Jonathan Lee has lucid dreamed, practiced body-attention work including qigong, and once experienced remaining subjectively aware while his body appeared to be asleep. He has not traveled through other realms and returned knowing with certainty that consciousness left the physical body. That unanswered question is part of why this book exists.

He is the author of the Psychical Excursion guidebook. The public website is psychicalexcursion.com.
